Black Friday · Serbia · 30-day lowest-price rule

Is that discount real?

Retailers must compare a discount with their lowest price in the previous 30 days. Our Mozaik agents check every advertised discount against the price history, cross-shop the same product, audit retailer trust and look for sealed listings on the marketplace — then tell you the cheapest honest way to buy.

The problem

A “before” price can be anything. Shops raise prices in early November, then advertise a huge cut on Black Friday. Serbian law says the reference for a price reduction is the trader’s lowest price in the period before the reduction — so the honest discount is measured from that low, not from the sticker.

How it works

Independent Mozaik agents react to shared events. Results stream in as each agent finishes; when a price changes mid-run, older answers are discarded. ShopTrust can rewrite the Verdict agent’s tool call while it is still running.

Retailer trust

ShopTrust audits every retailer’s Black Friday offers against their own 30-day minimums. Flagged retailers are removed from the recommendations — even when they are the cheapest.
